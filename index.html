Rumack-Matthew Nomogram Plotter

How to use: Enter the exact time that has elapsed since the acute ingestion. The nomogram is only valid for blood samples taken between 4 and 24 hours post-ingestion. Use the "Hours" and "Minutes" fields together to specify the time of the lab draw (e.g., for a draw at 4.5 hours, enter 4 in Hours and 30 in Minutes).
