Rumack-Matthew Nomogram Plotter

How to use: Confirm the clinical eligibility criteria below, then enter the exact time that has elapsed since the acute ingestion. The nomogram is only valid for blood samples taken between 4 and 24 hours post-ingestion. Use the "Hours" and "Minutes" fields together to specify the time of the lab draw (e.g., for a draw at 4.5 hours, enter 4 in Hours and 30 in Minutes).
Clinical Eligibility — Contraindications for Use
Single, acute ingestion? Ingestion occurred at a single point in time (not staggered, chronic, or repeated supratherapeutic ingestion).
Known time of ingestion? Time of ingestion is reliably known (if ingested over a brief window, use the earliest possible time).
Blood sample drawn 4 to 24 hours post-ingestion? Levels drawn <4 hours may not reflect peak absorption; levels >24 hours cannot be interpreted on this nomogram.
Cautionary Reminder — Delayed Absorption Risk
Extended-release formulation or gut-slowing co-ingestants? Modified/extended-release APAP or co-ingestion of opioids/anticholinergics that may delay absorption.
