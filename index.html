Rumack-Matthew Nomogram

Plots a serum acetaminophen level on the Rumack-Matthew nomogram,3 using the treatment and high-risk lines from the 2023 US and Canada consensus statement.1 Answer the questions below, then enter the time from the first ingestion to the blood draw and the level.
Nomogram eligibility
Acute ingestion? One or more ingestions, all within a 24-hour period. Ingestion over more than 24 hours is repeated supratherapeutic ingestion.1,2
Time of first ingestion known? Time is measured from the first ingestion. If it is only known within a short window, use the earliest possible time. If the history is unreliable, answer No.
Level drawn 4 to 24 hours after the first ingestion? A level drawn before 4 hours cannot be plotted. After 24 hours, the nomogram does not apply.
Delayed absorption
Extended-release product, or a co-ingested opioid or anticholinergic? Either can delay absorption. Extended-release means a product labeled for dosing every 8 hours.
